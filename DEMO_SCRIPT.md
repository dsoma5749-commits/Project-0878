# AgroPulse AI — Demo Script

## 1. Problem

Smallholder farmers face interconnected risks:
- climate uncertainty
- soil conditions
- crop disease
- market-price decisions
- flood and resilience risks

AgroPulse brings these signals into one decision-support interface.

## 2. Core Flow

Observe → Verify → Analyze → Recommend → Explain

## 3. Demo Sequence

### Step 1 — Overview
Show the AgroPulse dashboard and explain the unified agricultural-risk view.

### Step 2 — Climate Radar
Show climate and hydrological telemetry.
Explain that external data sources and observation windows are surfaced transparently.

### Step 3 — Soil Telemetry
Show simulated sensor-node telemetry and explain how soil signals can support irrigation and crop decisions.

### Step 4 — Vision Pathology
Upload/show a crop image.
Demonstrate the prototype computer-vision analysis and pathology visualization.

Important:
This is an AI-assisted prototype; production-grade trained-model inference is a future integration target.

### Step 5 — Mandi Arbitrage
Show market scenarios and explain how price and transport information can support market decisions.

### Step 6 — Farmer Resilience
Show the resilience loop and explain how multiple risks can be combined into actionable guidance.

### Step 7 — Judge Pitch
Open the Judge Pitch section.
Explain the problem, architecture, impact, technical approach and limitations.

## 4. Closing

AgroPulse is designed as a decision-support layer connecting environmental,
agricultural, market and resilience signals into one explainable workflow.

The prototype demonstrates the interface and decision architecture while
clearly distinguishing live data, calibrated data, simulated telemetry and
future integrations.
